Privacy Notice

Frijterz is designed for one person: its owner. Financial data is used only to display a private dashboard and is stored locally.

Effective 4 September 2026

In short
  • The bank connection is read-only.
  • Only account details and balances are requested initially.
  • Financial data is stored on the user's own device.
  • There are no advertisements, analytics, or tracking cookies.

1. About Frijterz

Frijterz is a private, non-commercial personal finance dashboard operated solely by its owner. It is not offered as a public banking or financial service. This notice describes how its optional bank connection handles information.

2. Information handled

When the bank connection is enabled, Frijterz may receive:

  • the financial institution and technical account identifier;
  • the account name or type, when supplied by the institution;
  • the account currency and current or available balance; and
  • authorization status and refresh timestamps.

The initial integration does not request transaction history and cannot initiate payments or transfers. Any future expansion of that scope will require renewed authorization and an updated notice.

3. Authentication and bank access

Account access is authorized through Enable Banking and the user's financial institution. Banking usernames, passwords, passcodes, and multifactor authentication secrets are entered only into interfaces supplied by those parties and are not received or stored by Frijterz. Frijterz stores only the technical authorization material needed to refresh the permitted read-only data.

4. Purpose

Information is used solely to retrieve and display the owner's bank balance alongside other personal financial information. It is not used for advertising, profiling, automated decision-making, or sale.

5. Storage and retention

Frijterz runs on the owner's computer. Retrieved financial data and authorization material are stored in local files protected by the device's user permissions. They are retained until replaced by a newer refresh or deleted by the owner. Copies may remain in device backups controlled by the owner until those backups expire.

This public legal website does not receive financial data and uses no analytics, forms, local storage, or non-essential cookies. Its hosting provider may process ordinary web-server logs under the provider's own privacy terms.

6. Disclosure and service providers

Frijterz does not sell personal information. Data is exchanged only with Enable Banking and the connected financial institution as needed to perform the authorized account-information request. Those parties process information under their own privacy notices and legal duties.

7. Security

The dashboard listens only on the local loopback interface, keeps secrets out of browser-visible dashboard responses, and restricts sensitive local files to the device owner. No security measure is absolute, so the owner is also responsible for device security, operating-system updates, and secure backups.

8. Control and deletion

The owner can stop processing at any time by disconnecting the bank authorization through the relevant bank or Enable Banking interface and deleting Frijterz's local authorization and balance files. The bank connection also expires when its authorization period ends unless it is renewed.

9. Changes to this notice

This notice will be revised before Frijterz requests additional bank data, adds cloud storage or analytics, or becomes available to other users. The effective date above identifies the current version.

10. Contact

Frijterz is a private, single-user application. Questions about this notice can be directed to the application owner through the contact information associated with its Enable Banking registration.
